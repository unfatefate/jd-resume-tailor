# JD And Industry Analysis

## Build a concise target profile

Extract the following before drafting:

- Job title, seniority, work location, business unit, and reporting context when available.
- Core responsibilities and their implied deliverables.
- Required versus preferred skills, tools, certifications, education, and experience.
- Metrics or operating outcomes: quality, delivery, cost, cycle time, conversion, safety, compliance, customer outcomes, and so on.
- Company product, customer, business model, operating stage, and industry-specific language.

Use a compact mapping while drafting:

| JD need | Candidate evidence | Strategy | Claim level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Requirement or implied need | Resume, attachments, user statement, or visible chat | Preserve, sharpen, extend, enhance, or invent | One of four levels |

## Industry adaptation

Use public company information and reputable sector sources when the company or industry changes the role's actual emphasis. Adapt terminology, workflow framing, and what appears first in the resume.

Examples:

- Consumer manufacturing: mass production, yield, takt, line balance, process stability, NPI, tooling, standard work.
- Logistics and supply chain: demand, plan, inventory, fulfillment, warehouse, transportation, service level, cost, data visibility.
- Software or internet products: user problems, delivery, experiments, data, cross-functional execution, product lifecycle.
- Regulated industries: traceability, risk, documentation, verification, compliance, quality systems.

Do not add words merely because they are common in an industry. A term belongs only when it improves the explanation of candidate evidence or is explicitly logged as enhanced or invented.

## Research boundaries

- Prefer the supplied JD, employer career site, official company materials, and reliable industry sources.
- Separate company and industry research from candidate evidence.
- Do not infer a candidate's experience, achievement, system access, certification, or numerical result from a company practice.
