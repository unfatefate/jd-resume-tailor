# Evidence And Claims

## Build the temporary evidence pool

Record the source of every meaningful fact from:

- Current user messages.
- Files and images attached in the current request.
- Current-thread history that is visible to the agent.
- User-authorized enhancement or invention instructions.

Do not claim access to invisible, prior-thread, or unavailable conversation history. When a fact is necessary but not visible, request it or mark the resulting content as invented.

## Claim levels

### original_fact

Directly stated or confirmed by the user or source material. Preserve exact numbers, dates, companies, and project names unless the user asks to change them.

### reasonable_extension

A direct role-oriented interpretation of a fact. Example: “compiled takt data” can be framed as “supported capacity and bottleneck analysis.” The underlying work must remain recognizable.

### enhanced_expression

A real prototype exists, but scope, method, context, ownership, or result is strengthened for the JD. State the prototype and strengthened elements in the internal attachment.

### invented_content

New content added for fit, including an experience, responsibility, tool use, project, outcome, metric, certification, or scenario. List it separately in the internal attachment with an interview-ready narrative.

## Numeric claims

- Retain source-backed numbers exactly unless the user directs otherwise.
- Any new number is `invented_content` or an estimate. Record the basis, unit, scope, and whether it must be removed before submission.
- Do not blend an invented number into a source-backed result without disclosure in the attachment.

## Internal ledger fields

For every material line in the tailored resume, record:

1. Final resume statement.
2. Resume module and role/project.
3. Source location or conversation summary.
4. Original prototype or source statement.
5. Claim level.
6. What changed and why it matches the JD or industry.
7. Interview risk and the detail the candidate must be able to explain.
