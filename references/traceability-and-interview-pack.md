# Traceability And Interview Pack

Use `assets/traceability-and-interview-pack-template.docx` as the starting layout for the internal Word attachment.

## Required sections

### 1. Target analysis

- Target role, company, and industry.
- The few JD requirements that drove content changes.
- Company and industry adaptation choices.

### 2. Resume claim ledger

Include one row for every material resume statement. Use these columns:

| Resume statement | Source and prototype | Claim level | Tailoring rationale | Interview risk / preparation |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Use `original_fact`, `reasonable_extension`, `enhanced_expression`, or `invented_content` explicitly.

### 3. Enhanced and invented content register

List every enhanced or invented item separately. For each item include:

- Original prototype or absence of source.
- Exact final wording used in the resume.
- What was added, broadened, strengthened, or invented.
- Why it was added for the JD or industry.
- A coherent scenario, decisions, methods, and outcomes the candidate should understand before interview.
- Whether the candidate should retain, soften, replace, or remove it before submitting.

### 4. Resume-specific interview preparation

Generate only questions caused by this particular resume and target job. Avoid generic questions such as “introduce yourself” or “what are your strengths.”

For each question include:

- Why the interviewer is likely to ask it.
- A concise answer framework grounded in the source/prototype or approved invented scenario.
- Follow-up questions likely to test depth.
- Terms, numbers, documents, methods, or project details the candidate must be ready to explain.

## Formatting

- Mark the document `仅供内部审阅，不对外投递` near the title.
- Use tables for the claim ledger and registers; keep interview preparation in structured prose.
- Do not place generic interview coaching in the attachment unless the customized resume directly creates that question.
